Elune M2 Blender add-on
Home Documentation

Elune M2 imports World of Warcraft M2 models from Classic to current retail, lets you edit their meshes, materials, geosets and animations, and exports.

Read the documentation Install guide

What it does

Import

Per-expansion parsers for every M2 version, with skeleton, animations, attachments, events, cameras and bounds.

Export

Build a standalone model, patch geometry into an existing slot, or write a retail character with its own .skel.

Materials

Texture types, FileDataIDs, hardcoded texture paths, blend modes and render flags from one panel.

Geosets

Assign hair, armor and equipment geoset ids by group and variant, one mesh or many at a time.

Animation

Round-trip hundreds of sequences, tune blend times, re-pose a rig without breaking its clips.

Physics

Rig cloth, chains and soft body parts in one click, preview the motion on the skeleton, tune it, and export it inside the model.
